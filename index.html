"Growth isn't the problem. Extraction is. We're building a new model of progress: one rooted in reciprocity, access, and imagination."

A Regenerative Ecosystem

Sana Growth operates as a holding studio—a multidisciplinary infrastructure that bridges strategic advisory, venture investment, and experimental design. Each project, partnership, and venture cross-pollinates with the others, creating a living system where insights from one domain strengthen the whole.

We work at the intersection of urgency and imagination: supporting founders building regenerative businesses, advising organizations through systemic transformation, and prototyping new models for community, equity, and belonging.
